# IronAlign (hosted copy)

A built, static copy of IronAlign, a local-first gym and posture coach. Open it at
https://botbender167.github.io/ironalign-coach-site/

Everything runs in your browser and is stored on your device (IndexedDB). There is no account and no server.
The AI coach and AI food estimates need the full app with its AI router, so they are switched off here.
